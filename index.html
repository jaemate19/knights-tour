♞ KNIGHT'S TOUR ♞

📜 RULES

  • The Knight moves in an "L" shape: 2 squares one way, 1 square perpendicular
  • Each square can be visited only once
  • Win by visiting all 64 squares

    • 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

  • Make legal moves until you win or reach a dead end
  • Use Hint to see possible moves
